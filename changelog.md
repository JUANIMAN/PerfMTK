# Changelog

## v16.2

> [!NOTE]
> This release marks a massive upgrade for PerfMTK, bringing together all features, improvements, and optimizations developed since version 15.2 into a single, polished release.

### Highlights (What's New)

* **Brand New Native Engine**:
  * Completely rewritten from scratch in high-performance native code to replace legacy scripts.
  * Ultra-lightweight: runs quietly in the background using virtually zero battery and under 3 MB of RAM.
  * Instant performance profile switching with zero delay when launching or switching apps.

* **Rock-Solid Gaming & Stutter-Free Experience**:
  * Tuned for high-refresh-rate gaming (stable 90–120 FPS in demanding games like PUBG Mobile, Warzone, and Genshin Impact).
  * Smarter workload management ensures screen rendering stays buttery smooth and free of micro-stutters.

* **Ultra-Responsive Touch**:
  * Automatically unlocks higher touch response rates on supported screens for lightning-fast aiming, tapping, and gesture tracking.
  * Smoother, more accurate touch input with reduced input latency.

* **Charge Bypass & Battery Care**:
  * **Cooler Gaming While Charging**: Safely bypasses battery heat generation during heavy gaming sessions, keeping your phone cool in your hands.
  * **Battery Health Protection**: Set custom maximum charge limits (such as 80%) to prolong your battery's lifespan over time.
  * **Thermal Safety Guard**: Automatically protects your device from dangerous battery temperatures.

* **Smart Thermal Guardian**:
  * Proactive temperature management prevents sudden, jarring FPS drops (thermal throttling) during long gaming sessions.
  * Gradually balances performance and heat so your games stay smooth without drastic frame dips.

* **Universal MediaTek Device Support**:
  * Fully compatible with both Dimensity and Helio chipsets.
  * Works seamlessly across major Android skins (Xiaomi HyperOS / MIUI, OnePlus / OPPO / Realme ColorOS, Vivo OriginOS, Samsung One UI, and custom AOSP ROMs).

* **Revamped Companion App (PerfMTK-Manager)**:
  * Sleek, modern dashboard showing live CPU, GPU, memory, and temperature stats.
  * Easy-to-use sliders and toggles to customize your performance profiles and battery settings.
  * Handy Quick Settings tiles to switch profiles or toggle Charge Bypass directly from your notification panel.

* **Improved Terminal Menu**:
  * Running `su -c perfmtk` now opens a clear, interactive dashboard with easy-to-navigate menus in English.
